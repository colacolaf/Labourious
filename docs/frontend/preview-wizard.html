How should Labourious pay for runs?

Free-only stays local forever. Hybrid routes just the final synthesis to a paid model — best of both, costs cents per run.

●Free-only $0 Every agent on Ollama. Zero cost; quality depends on model size.
○Hybrid ~$0.05/run Free for research; Claude Sonnet synthesises the final memo. Costs ~5¢/run.
○All-paid ~$0.20/run Every agent on Claude Sonnet. Best quality; ~20¢ per f1 memo.
Pick Free-only to start
  • You can switch any time with /paid-for final-report for hybrid, or /model anthropic/claude-sonnet-4-5 for all-paid.
  • Hybrid is recommended once you've seen a few free memos and trust the citations.
  • Footer always shows live cost: free · 5 agents or ≈ $0.05 · 5 agents.
